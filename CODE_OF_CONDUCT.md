# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Project participants must communicate respectfully, welcome constructive
technical disagreement, avoid harassment and discrimination, and protect
private or sensitive information shared during collaboration.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include personal attacks, sexualized or
discriminatory language, sustained disruption, intimidation, doxxing, and
publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens a safe,
professional community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/MilanoTechnicalGroupInc/isaac-sim-gaussian-lod/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `Code of Conduct`. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible.
